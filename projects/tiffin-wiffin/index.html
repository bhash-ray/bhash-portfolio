Research

Where it began

The initial inspiration came from observing how mothers feed their fussy children and the different ways they encourage them to finish their meals. Personal experiences of coaxing my nephews into eating also influenced the concept.

Concept

Tiffin Wiffin is an applied game about toddler nutrition, developed for the Science Gallery Bengaluru exhibition CALORIE. The game explores the relationship between calorie intake and nutritional value through an everyday parenting challenge: feeding a fussy toddler before mealtime runs out.

Inspired by watching mothers coax their children into finishing their meals, and my own experiences of trying to get my fussy nephews to eat, the game turns the familiar chaos of mealtime into a playful challenge.

Players assemble a meal, choose the appropriate cutlery and feed the toddler one bite at a time. When the toddler refuses food, players must use different coaxing techniques to encourage them to eat.

Core Idea

“Eating enough calories is not the same as receiving adequate nutrition.”

The Design Challenge

“Can you meet the calorie target while making nutritious food choices, within two minutes?”

The Design Approach

Rather than presenting nutritional information through charts or instructions, Tiffin Wiffin uses interactive gameplay to communicate the idea. The central design objective was to make players experience the difference between meeting a calorie target and making nutritious food choices.

Core Loop

Perfect
More than 300 kcal, with less than half of the total calories coming from Cerelac.The toddler celebrates with a big YAY!“Calories and nutrition were met.”
Nutrition Missed
More than 300 kcal, but half or more of the total calories come from Cerelac.The toddler becomes sick.“Energy was filled, but nutrition was missed. The baby fell sick.”
Time Out
300 kcal or less when the timer ends.The toddler becomes sick.“Feeding time slipped away. Babies need to be fed on time.”

Endings

The meal is evaluated when the timer ends. Each ending has its
own visual feedback and a change in background music.

How to Play

Visitors should be able to walk up and play without anyone explaining the rules. A five-step illustrated tutorial plays before every round. Scroll, swipe or click through the steps.

Coaxing Tricks

Refusal is a central part of the gameplay. Each bite has a 35% chance of being accepted immediately. Otherwise, the baby asks for one of three coaxing techniques, depending on the food.

Future Scope

The current prototype focuses on the toddler stage. The larger vision is an experience that grows with the child, from infancy to six years.
